A new icon is in your menu bar and the service has started. No restart needed.

One panel. Three modes.
Click the sun or moon icon to open Lidlight. Choose Automatic, Stay awake or Allow sleep. Details and Copy diagnostics are available in the same panel.

To uninstall
Run this in Terminal. Sleep settings are restored too:
sudo /Applications/Lidlight.app/Contents/Resources/uninstall.sh
