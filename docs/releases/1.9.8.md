# Lidlight 1.9.8

Battery charging controls are now available in More → Power & display on Macs with an internal battery.

- On supported older Apple Silicon systems, opt in to an approximate 80% charge limit; charging resumes at 75%.
- Charge to 100% once, then return to the limit. The override ends at full charge, unplugging, service restart or 24 hours.
- On Apple Silicon running macOS 26.4 or later, open the native Battery settings to configure Apple's charge limit. Lidlight does not read or change that native setting.
- Hide charging controls on desktops. Report unsupported hardware or firmware explicitly; Intel charging control is not supported.
- Verify charge-gate writes, retain recovery state across crashes, and restore Lidlight-owned charging on disable, service shutdown and uninstall. Uninstall stops if recovery fails.
- Pause ordinary limited charging before sleep, even below 80%, then reevaluate on wake. Charging while powered off is not controlled.

The direct SMC backend is opt-in and has passed simulated control/recovery tests, but physical charging behavior has not yet been verified on real hardware. Avoid running multiple charging controllers together.
