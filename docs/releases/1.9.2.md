## Fixes

- Switching update channels while downloading no longer opens an installer from the old channel. The new channel is checked afterward.
- Correcting the system clock backward no longer stalls performance sampling or automatic update checks. Future-dated chart points are removed; quota polling uses monotonic uptime.
- Cancelling a presentation or changing sessions invalidates delayed activation and screen-off actions.
- Process watching is registered only after its awake timer starts successfully. Cancelling a session clears pending state immediately.
- Disabling and re-enabling quota ignores obsolete in-flight responses and performs a fresh check.
- Quota parsing rejects boolean fields, fractional durations and extreme reset dates.
- Quota recovery tracking resets incompatible evidence after a window-duration change, without falsely announcing recovery.
- Reopening Performance starts a fresh CPU baseline instead of including time spent off the page.

## Efficiency

- Hardware sampling runs only on the home and Performance pages while displays are awake.
- Cat animation frames reuse cached images without rebuilding tooltip strings every frame.
- Redundant empty process-state updates and unchanged menu-bar title assignments are avoided.

## Validation

All 16 test suites passed during the audit, covering updates, quota, countdowns, performance, sessions, power policy, diagnostics, safe quit, CLI, daemon dry-run and mocked uninstall. Package version, universal executable and app/helper signatures were verified, and native diagnostics mode completed successfully.
