## MagSafe blinking reliability

- Keep the amber/off blink on a monotonic one-second cadence. Five-second app heartbeats renew the control lease without toggling the LED a second time.
- Recover from a transient firmware/adapter LED reset instead of permanently stopping after a single unexpected value.
- Stop after three consecutive external overrides to avoid fighting another LED utility. The conflict message now remains visible until the feature is disabled and re-enabled.
- Regression tests cover repeated heartbeats, stable blink edges, transient reset recovery, persistent conflicts, lease expiry and restart recovery.

Compatible Apple Silicon MagSafe 3 hardware only. Physical light behavior still requires confirmation on the connected cable. The installer remains unsigned and not Apple notarized.

Additional correction: use the standard AppleSMC LED connection, require a successful write response, allow a short read-back settling period, and treat “system” as a handoff command rather than requiring the firmware to report a zero color.
