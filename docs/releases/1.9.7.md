# Lidlight 1.9.7

A clearer dashboard with softer frosted glass and faster access to everyday controls.

- Increase background frosting to reduce wallpaper interference, with adaptive light and dark surfaces.
- Soften reflective borders and use more substantial glass controls.
- Switch between Automatic, Stay awake and Allow sleep directly on the home page.
- Keep Codex quota visible at the top and give Countdown a dedicated home shortcut.
- Simplify tool navigation; move diagnostics, updates and additional power controls into More.
- Keep update and service warnings visible, and hide fan readings on fanless Macs.

macOS 12 or newer. Universal Intel and Apple Silicon package.
The app is ad-hoc signed; the installer is unsigned and not Apple-notarized.
