# Lidlight 1.9.4

Broader Mac sensor compatibility on macOS 12 and newer (Intel and Apple Silicon).

- Discover Intel CPU core and dual-package thermal channels from the SMC inventory, excluding integrated GPU, battery and board sensors.
- Enable optional CPU-named HID thermal fallback across all Apple Silicon generations, keeping valid SMC readings primary.
- Recover sensor connections after repeated failures; cache missing keys briefly to avoid repeated unsuccessful calls.
- Support additional signed fixed-point formats used on older Intel systems, and hide fan metrics on fanless 12-inch MacBooks.
- Match Apple chip families exactly, so future M10 hardware cannot inherit M1 mappings.

The package remains universal arm64/x86_64 with macOS 12 minimum. Live sensor readings were verified on M2 Pro; Intel, M1 and other models need physical confirmation. Unavailable whole-system power/CPU temperature remains unavailable rather than estimated or mislabeled. This does not claim every sensor works on every Mac.

The app is ad-hoc signed; the installer is unsigned and not Apple-notarized.
