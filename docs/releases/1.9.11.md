## Gentler Codex activity light

The MagSafe activity indicator now stays amber for two seconds and off for two seconds. This is a slower blink, not hardware brightness fading. Idle remains green; immediate lights-off and the night schedule still take priority.

Includes the LED reliability and system-handoff corrections prepared in 1.9.10. Compatible MagSafe 3 hardware only. Installer unsigned and not Apple notarized.
