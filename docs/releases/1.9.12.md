## Activity light during quiet hours

Codex activity now pulses amber for two seconds on and two seconds off even when idle-off or night mode is enabled. When activity finishes, the light turns off during either darkness mode, otherwise it returns to green. Disable Show Codex activity to keep darkness unconditional.

Includes the MagSafe reliability corrections and slower pulse from 1.9.10–1.9.11. Compatible MagSafe 3 hardware only. Installer unsigned and not Apple notarized.
